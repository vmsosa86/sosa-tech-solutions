## 1. Objective

Build a consistent, credible brand and content system that turns the Sosa Tech Solutions website, Facebook page, and Instagram account into a lead-generation engine.

The work should make it immediately clear:

1. Who Sosa Tech helps.
2. What business problems Sosa Tech solves.
3. Why a prospect should trust Sosa Tech.
4. What the prospect should do next.

The primary business result is not follower count. It is more qualified WhatsApp conversations, discovery calls, and project inquiries.

## 2. Current Brand Audit

### What should be retained

- The circuit-style **S** mark is distinctive, recognizable at small sizes, and connected to the technology category.
- The core palette—electric teal, charcoal, and off-white—feels modern and technical.
- **We build. You grow.** is concise, memorable, and flexible enough to remain the master tagline.
- The current typography system—Syne, DM Sans, and DM Mono—already creates a useful contrast between bold marketing language and technical detail.
- The bilingual, Miami-rooted positioning is a real differentiator.
- The existing site has a strong visual foundation, clear contact paths, legal pages, and useful long-form articles.

### Problems to solve

- Seven services are presented with equal importance, which makes the company feel broad rather than specialized.
- The site describes capabilities but shows little proof: no case studies, before/after examples, testimonials, client outcomes, or founder story.
- Some website metadata and claims require cleanup or verification.
- The Facebook and Instagram profiles need a complete launch system: profile copy, cover art, highlights, pinned content, templates, and a sustainable publishing cadence.
- The current visual system relies on emojis in service cards; branded icons would make it look more intentional.
- Some color combinations fail normal-text accessibility. For example, `#5A6A7A` on `#080A0F` is approximately 3.56:1, and teal `#00E5C8` on white is approximately 1.46:1.

## 3. Recommended Brand Strategy

### Provisional brand foundation

These recommendations should be confirmed during the first working session and then locked in the brand manual.

**Mission**  
Help small businesses use practical technology to capture more opportunities, eliminate repetitive work, and operate with confidence.

**Vision**  
Become Miami's most trusted bilingual technology partner for small businesses that need enterprise-quality systems without enterprise complexity or cost.

**Brand promise**  
Useful technology, built clearly, deployed reliably, and tied to a real business result.

**Personality**  
Capable, direct, resourceful, modern, and approachable.

**Positioning statement**  
For Miami and South Florida small businesses that have outgrown manual processes or disconnected tools, Sosa Tech Solutions builds websites, automation, AI, and infrastructure that work together. Unlike large agencies or one-size-fits-all software vendors, Sosa Tech provides direct, bilingual support and practical systems sized for the business.

### Recommended offer architecture

The website and social profiles should lead with three commercial outcomes rather than seven unrelated technologies:

1. **Launch & Convert** — websites, landing pages, lead capture, and conversion tracking.
2. **Automate & Respond** — WhatsApp automation, CRM workflows, n8n, AI assistants, and follow-up systems.
3. **Reach & Grow** — Meta/Google advertising, campaign creative, and lead-generation funnels.

Two specialist capabilities can remain visible but secondary:

- **Run Reliably** — VPS, Docker, Cloudflare, monitoring, and managed infrastructure.
- **Stream & Broadcast** — radio and live-streaming infrastructure.

Tech consulting becomes the entry point used to diagnose the right solution, not another equal service card.

### Priority audiences

**Primary audience**  
Owner-operated service businesses in Miami and South Florida, typically 2–50 employees, that receive leads through a website, Instagram, Facebook, phone, or WhatsApp and lose time to manual follow-up.

**Secondary audience**  
Spanish-speaking and bilingual entrepreneurs who need a hands-on technology partner rather than a large agency.

**Specialist audience**  
Radio, media, and content businesses that need stable streaming, hosting, and automation.

### Brand message hierarchy

- **Master tagline:** We build. You grow.
- **Core message:** Technology that captures leads, saves time, and keeps your business moving.
- **Proof message:** Direct bilingual support, transparent scopes, efficient tools, and systems built around the client's actual workflow.
- **Primary CTA:** Book a free systems review.
- **Secondary CTA:** Message us on WhatsApp.

## 4. Brand Manual Deliverable

Create a comprehensive brand manual, plus a short quick-reference sheet. The final package should include:

### Brand foundation

- Mission, vision, promise, values, personality, and positioning.
- Primary and secondary audiences.
- Offer architecture and brand story.
- Competitive frame and differentiation.

### Logo system

- Primary horizontal logo, stacked logo, icon-only mark, and reversed versions.
- Clear-space rule based on one circuit-node diameter.
- Minimum sizes for web, social, and print.
- Approved background combinations.
- Misuse examples: distortion, recoloring, shadows, effects, busy backgrounds, and AI-redrawn versions.
- Profile-avatar treatment using the icon only, with enough padding to survive circular cropping.

### Color system

Begin with the colors already present in the site and logo:

| Role | Starting color | Current use |
|---|---:|---|
| Electric Teal | `#00E5C8` | Accent, action, logo |
| Carbon | `#080A0F` | Primary dark background |
| Deep Surface | `#0D1117` | Section background |
| Graphite Card | `#131820` | Card background |
| Circuit Border | `#1E2731` | Rules and borders |
| Signal White | `#F0F4F8` | Primary light text |
| Steel | `#5A6A7A` | Secondary text; must be adjusted for small copy |

The manual will add:

- An accessible dark teal, such as `#00796B`, for teal text on light backgrounds.
- A brighter secondary-text neutral for dark backgrounds.
- RGB, CMYK, and closest practical print references.
- Contrast-tested digital combinations and prohibited combinations.
- A usage ratio so teal remains a recognizable accent rather than overwhelming every design.

### Typography

- **Display:** Syne 700/800.
- **Body/UI:** DM Sans 400/500.
- **Technical accent:** DM Mono 400/500.
- Fallback font stacks, hierarchy, line height, letter spacing, mobile rules, and social-template sizes.
- A rule that technical styling supports clarity; it must not make normal business copy feel like code.

### Imagery, iconography, and motion

- Real workflows, hands-on implementation, screens, small-business settings, and Miami context.
- Clean isometric or editorial technology illustrations with dark surfaces, teal signal paths, soft grid structures, and controlled glow.
- A custom outline icon family to replace unrelated emojis.
- Motion rules for short UI traces, node connections, and before/after transitions.
- Avoid generic robots, blue holograms, fake dashboards, cyberpunk clichés, and AI-generated text inside images.

### Verbal identity

- Plainspoken, confident, useful, and specific.
- Lead with the business problem and result; explain technology second.
- Use short sentences and active voice.
- Avoid inflated claims such as “revolutionary,” “game-changing,” “guaranteed,” or unexplained technical jargon.
- English and Spanish guidance should preserve meaning and tone instead of using literal word-for-word translation.

### Brand in use

- Website, social posts, reels, stories, covers, proposals, case studies, email signatures, and presentations.
- Caption formula, CTA library, hashtag/location guidance, subtitle style, and thumbnail rules.
- Asset naming, version control, approval owner, and source-file location.

## 8. Kie API Content Production Workflow

Kie API should accelerate production without being allowed to redefine the brand on every generation.

### Recommended pipeline

```text
Content brief
  → approved script/hook
  → brand prompt assembled from fixed tokens
  → Kie API generates 3 visual candidates
  → deterministic logo, headline, and subtitles added
  → brand/accuracy review
  → Victor approval
  → Meta draft/publish
  → metrics captured for the next iteration
```

### Rules

- Store Kie credentials only in a server-side environment file; never in browser code, prompts, or the repository.
- Version the prompt template and save the prompt, model, aspect ratio, and output ID with each asset.
- Use Kie primarily for backgrounds, illustrations, storyboards, B-roll concepts, and visual variations.
- Add the real logo and all important text after generation using a deterministic template. Do not trust image generation to spell the brand, phone number, CTA, or technical UI correctly.
- Generate a high-resolution master and export channel-specific versions. Initial targets are 1080×1350 feed, 1080×1080 square, 1080×1920 reel/story, and 1200×630 link/cover; re-check platform requirements before production.
- Require a human approval checkpoint before publishing until the visual system and content accuracy are consistently reliable.
- Preserve usage rights, source images, client permission, and disclosure records where applicable.

### Brand QA checklist for generated assets

- Approved palette and type hierarchy.
- Correct, unaltered logo with clear space.
- One clear focal point and one message.
- Text contrast and mobile readability.
- No fake client results, fake interfaces, extra fingers, invented logos, or inaccurate technical diagrams.
- Correct English/Spanish spelling.
- Reel-safe zones respected for captions and UI overlays.

## 9. Measurement

### Primary business metrics

- Qualified WhatsApp conversations.
- Discovery calls booked.
- Contact forms completed.
- Proposals and won projects attributed to content or ads.

### Content diagnostics

- Reel watch time, completion rate, and first-three-second retention.
- Saves, shares, meaningful comments, and profile visits.
- Website and WhatsApp clicks per post.
- Carousel reach to final-slide views when available.
- Performance by pillar, language, hook, and CTA—not just by format.

### Review rhythm

- Weekly: identify the strongest hook and weakest drop-off.
- Monthly: review leads and content performance; keep, change, or stop each recurring format.
- At 90 days: update the brand/content playbook using real audience behavior.

The first 30 days establish a baseline. Do not invent arbitrary follower targets before the accounts have enough current data.

## 10. 90-Day Execution Roadmap

### Days 1–5 — foundation and proof inventory

- Confirm the primary audience, three lead offers, and preferred CTA.
- Inventory completed projects, screenshots, measurable outcomes, and testimonial permissions.
- Confirm founder-led vs. studio-led presentation. Recommended default: founder-led trust with a scalable studio brand.
- Approve the provisional mission, positioning, personality, and message hierarchy.

**Done when:** one positioning statement, one message hierarchy, three primary offers, and at least three proof candidates are approved.

### Days 6–10 — brand manual and templates

- Finalize logo usage, color accessibility, typography, image style, iconography, voice, and bilingual rules.
- Produce the full manual and quick-reference sheet.
- Create profile, cover, carousel, reel-cover, story, quote, and case-study templates.
- Define the Kie prompt system and approval checklist.

**Done when:** a new designer or writer can produce an on-brand asset without asking for undocumented rules.

### Days 11–20 — profile and website conversion update

- Complete Facebook and Instagram profile setup.
- Publish and pin the three foundation posts.
- Implement Priority 0 website fixes.
- Rewrite the hero and offer architecture.
- Add the first proof section and improve tracking.

**Done when:** each social profile and the homepage communicate the same audience, promise, offers, proof, and CTA.

### Days 21–30 — first content batch

- Produce 12 feed assets, including at least 6 reels.
- Prepare bilingual captions, thumbnails, CTAs, UTMs, and story derivatives.
- Schedule only approved assets.
- Capture baseline performance and lead data.

**Done when:** four weeks of content can publish consistently without last-minute creation.

### Days 31–60 — publish and prove

- Publish the weekly cadence.
- Add one detailed case study and one primary-offer landing page.
- Convert existing blog articles into reels, carousels, and email/social snippets.
- Test hooks, reel length, language, CTA, and founder-led content.

### Days 61–90 — optimize and scale

- Build the remaining primary-offer pages.
- Turn the best organic themes into paid creative tests.
- Automate reporting and content-status tracking with n8n.
- Document winning hooks and retire weak recurring formats.
- Update the manual where real production exposed missing rules.

## 11. Recommended Starting Decisions

Unless business priorities indicate otherwise, begin with these defaults:

- Lead category: **small-business automation and lead systems**.
- Supporting offer: **conversion-focused websites**.
- Third offer: **paid acquisition only after tracking and follow-up are ready**.
- Voice: expert but plainspoken; no agency hype.
- Brand model: founder-led credibility under the Sosa Tech Solutions identity.
- Geography: Miami/South Florida first, remote clients second.
- Language: bilingual captions; alternate spoken-language reels.
- CTA: free systems review, with WhatsApp as the fastest path.

These defaults make the brand more memorable without removing the wider technical capabilities already present in the business.
