- Brand: Sosa Tech Solutions / Sosa Tech.
- Tagline: **We build. You grow.**
- Market: Miami and South Florida; English and Spanish.
- Positioning: a direct bilingual technology partner for small businesses.
- Primary offers: Launch & Convert; Automate & Respond; Reach & Grow.
- Specialist offers: Run Reliably; Stream & Broadcast.
- Personality: capable, direct, resourceful, modern, approachable.

- Voice: lead with the business problem and outcome; explain technology second.
- Primary CTA: Book a free systems review.
- Secondary CTA: Message us on WhatsApp.
