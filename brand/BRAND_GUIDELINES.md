# Sosa Tech Solutions Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0 working edition  
**Brand guardian:** Victor Sosa  
**Primary market:** Miami and South Florida  
**Languages:** English and Spanish  
**Master tagline:** **We build. You grow.**

## 1. About This Guide

This guide is the working source of truth for anyone designing, writing, publishing, or building for Sosa Tech Solutions. It applies to the website, social media, advertising, proposals, presentations, email, and print. Use the exact logo files, colors, type hierarchy, voice, and messaging rules defined here. When a new application is not covered, choose the solution that is clearest, most useful, and least decorative, then document the decision for the next revision.

## 2. Brand Foundation

### Mission

Help small businesses use practical technology to capture more opportunities, eliminate repetitive work, and operate with confidence.

### Vision

Become Miami's most trusted bilingual technology partner for small businesses that need enterprise-quality systems without enterprise complexity or cost.

### Brand promise

Useful technology, built clearly, deployed reliably, and tied to a real business result.

### Values

1. **Clarity over complexity** — Explain what is being built, why it matters, and what it costs in language a business owner can use.
2. **Execution over theater** — Favor working systems, measured improvements, and visible progress over impressive-sounding presentations.
3. **Ownership over dependency** — Build maintainable systems and make responsibilities, access, and handoff clear.
4. **Efficiency with judgment** — Use automation and AI where they save time or improve service, not simply because they are fashionable.
5. **Local trust, professional standards** — Deliver direct bilingual support with the rigor expected from a serious technology partner.

### Personality

**Capable · Direct · Resourceful · Modern · Approachable**

### Brand idea

Sosa Tech connects the moving parts of a small business—attention, leads, conversations, workflows, websites, and infrastructure—so they work as one useful system.

### Positioning statement

For Miami and South Florida small businesses that have outgrown manual processes or disconnected tools, Sosa Tech Solutions builds websites, automation, AI, and infrastructure that work together. Unlike large agencies or one-size-fits-all software vendors, Sosa Tech provides direct, bilingual support and practical systems sized for the business.

### Audiences

**Primary**  
Owner-operated service businesses with 2–50 employees that receive inquiries through a website, Facebook, Instagram, phone, or WhatsApp and lose time or opportunities to manual follow-up.

**Secondary**  
Spanish-speaking and bilingual entrepreneurs who want a hands-on technology partner rather than a large agency.

**Specialist**  
Radio, media, and content organizations that require stable streaming, hosting, and automation.

### Offer architecture

Lead with outcomes, then explain the technology.

1. **Launch & Convert** — websites, landing pages, lead capture, and conversion tracking.
2. **Automate & Respond** — WhatsApp automation, CRM workflows, n8n, AI assistants, and follow-up systems.
3. **Reach & Grow** — Meta/Google advertising, campaign creative, and lead-generation funnels.
4. **Run Reliably** — VPS, Docker, Cloudflare, monitoring, and managed infrastructure.
5. **Stream & Broadcast** — radio and live-streaming infrastructure.

The first three are the primary commercial story. The last two are specialist capabilities. Tech consulting is the diagnostic entry point, not a sixth equal offer.

## 3. Logo System

### Logo idea

The mark is a circuit-path **S** built from connected lines and nodes. It represents connection, movement, practical infrastructure, and systems that work together. The wordmark balances a bold business name with a precise technical accent.

### Approved current assets

| Use | Asset |
|---|---|
| Scalable master | `assets/logo.svg` |
| Dark-background lockup | `assets/logo-dark.png` |
| Light-background lockup | `assets/logo-light.png` |
| Icon/profile source | `assets/whatsapp-profile-icon.png` |

Do not recreate the logo with an image-generation model. Always place the approved asset over the final composition.

### Variations

- **Primary dark lockup:** Electric Teal mark and `TECH`, Signal White `SOSA`, Steel `SOLUTIONS` on Carbon or Deep Surface.
- **Primary light lockup:** Electric Teal mark and `TECH`, Carbon `SOSA`, Steel `SOLUTIONS` on Signal White.
- **Icon-only:** Circuit **S** centered in a square or circular-crop-safe field. Use for profile images, avatars, favicons, and small UI applications.
- **One-color:** Carbon on a light background or Signal White on a dark background when color reproduction is unavailable.
- **Stacked lockup:** Not approved in v1. Create and review a real asset before using one.

### Clear space

Let **N** equal the diameter of one circular node in the circuit mark. Keep at least **2N** of empty space around the complete logo and **3N** around the icon when used as a social avatar. No text, border, photo detail, or other logo may enter this area.

### Minimum size

| Version | Digital | Print |
|---|---:|---:|
| Full horizontal lockup | 160 px wide | 42 mm wide |
| Icon-only | 24 px wide | 8 mm wide |
| Social profile source | 512×512 px preferred | N/A |

At sizes below 160 px, use the icon instead of shrinking the wordmark until `SOLUTIONS` becomes illegible.

### Background control

- Prefer Carbon, Deep Surface, or Signal White.
- On photography, place the logo in a solid Carbon or Signal White container.
- Maintain strong contrast and keep visual detail outside the clear-space area.
- Teal logo elements may not sit directly on similarly bright green, aqua, or cyan areas.

### Incorrect use

- Do not stretch, compress, rotate, skew, or redraw the mark.
- Do not change individual logo colors outside the approved variants.
- Do not add drop shadows, bevels, outlines, gradients, glow, or animation inside the logo.
- Do not place the logo directly on a busy image.
- Do not typeset `SOSATECH` as a substitute for the wordmark.
- Do not separate circuit nodes or change line thickness.
- Do not use an AI-generated approximation of the mark.
- Do not place the tagline so close that it becomes part of the logo lockup unless using the approved exported artwork.

CMYK values are practical starting conversions and must be proofed for the actual printer and material. No Pantone spot color is assigned in v1; match by physical proof before declaring one.

### Supporting colors

| Name | Hex | RGB | CMYK | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| Graphite Card | `#131820` | 19, 24, 32 | 41, 25, 0, 87 | Cards and panels |

| Deep Teal | `#00796B` | 0, 121, 107 | 100, 0, 12, 53 | Teal text on light backgrounds |

### Color ratio

For a typical dark composition:

- 70% Carbon, Deep Surface, or Graphite.
- 20% Signal White and Clear Steel.
- 10% Electric Teal.

Teal is a signal: use it for the most important word, path, state, or action. If everything is teal, nothing is emphasized.

### Approved digital combinations

- Signal White on Carbon: approximately 17.91:1.
- Electric Teal on Carbon: approximately 12.30:1.
- Carbon on Electric Teal: approximately 12.30:1.
- Clear Steel on Carbon: approximately 7.49:1.
- Deep Teal on Signal White: approximately 4.81:1.

### Restricted combinations

- Electric Teal on Signal White is approximately 1.46:1 and cannot be used for normal text.
- Steel on Carbon is approximately 3.56:1 and cannot be used for small normal text. Use Clear Steel.
- Circuit Border is decorative on dark surfaces and cannot carry essential information by itself.
- Never use color alone to indicate success, failure, selected state, or navigation position.

## 5. Typography

### Typefaces

- **Syne 700/800:** display headlines, campaign statements, and large numerals.
- **DM Sans 400/500:** body copy, forms, navigation, captions, proposals, and UI.
- **DM Mono 400/500:** labels, eyebrow text, technical annotations, metadata, and small accent phrases.

### Fallback stacks

```css
--font-display: "Syne", "Arial Black", Arial, sans-serif;
--font-body: "DM Sans", Inter, Arial, sans-serif;
--font-mono: "DM Mono", "Courier New", monospace;
```

### Website hierarchy

| Level | Typeface | Weight | Size | Line height | Use |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Display/H1 | Syne | 800 | 48–88 px | 0.95–1.02 | One primary page statement |
| H2 | Syne | 700/800 | 36–64 px | 1.00–1.08 | Section headline |
| H3 | Syne | 700 | 24–36 px | 1.10–1.20 | Offer or card title |
| Body large | DM Sans | 400 | 18–22 px | 1.45–1.60 | Introductions |
| Body | DM Sans | 400 | 16–18 px | 1.50–1.65 | General copy |
| Caption | DM Sans | 400/500 | 13–15 px | 1.40–1.55 | Supporting text |
| Label | DM Mono | 500 | 11–14 px | 1.20–1.40 | Tags and system labels |

### Social hierarchy at 1080 px width

| Level | Typeface | Weight | Starting size |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Main hook | Syne | 800 | 96–132 px |
| Supporting statement | DM Sans | 500 | 44–58 px |
| Body/checklist | DM Sans | 400/500 | 38–48 px |
| Label/step | DM Mono | 500 | 26–34 px |

Always test at phone size. Reduce the number of words before reducing type below a comfortable reading size.

### Typography rules

- Use sentence case for normal headlines. All caps are reserved for short DM Mono labels.
- Use tight display leading and normal body leading.
- Do not justify paragraphs.
- Keep body lines around 50–75 characters on the website.
- Avoid more than three font weights in one layout.
- Never use DM Mono for paragraphs or emotional customer copy.
- Highlight no more than one key phrase in Electric Teal per headline.

## 6. Imagery and Graphic Language

### Photography

Show real people working, real Miami businesses, hands-on implementation, useful screens, and direct collaboration. Images should feel observant and capable, not staged like a large corporate office.

Preferred treatment:

- Natural contrast with controlled cool shadows.
- Honest skin tones.
- Dark surfaces with restrained teal environmental accents.
- Clean negative space for short overlay copy.
- Crops that show action: discussing, building, testing, measuring, or serving a customer.

### Illustration

Use clean editorial or isometric system diagrams:

- Carbon or Deep Surface base.
- Fine Circuit Border grid.
- Electric Teal paths and active nodes.
- Signal White labels.
- One clear flow from input to outcome.
- Soft light, limited depth, and controlled glow.

### Kie/image-generation prompt core

> Premium editorial technology illustration for a bilingual Miami small-business technology consultancy. Carbon-black background, restrained electric-teal signal paths, graphite panels, subtle technical grid, clean negative space, realistic depth, precise modern lighting, one clear focal point, practical business technology rather than science fiction, no typography, no logos, no watermark.

Append the specific business scene and aspect ratio to this fixed core. Add all important text and the real logo afterward in a controlled template.

### Avoid

- Humanoid robots and glowing robot heads.
- Generic handshakes, call-center stock photos, or fake boardrooms.
- Blue hologram interfaces floating in mid-air.
- Dense cyberpunk scenes, green “Matrix” code, or hacker imagery.
- Fake charts, invented performance numbers, or unreadable dashboards.
- Generated text, logos, URLs, phone numbers, or client names.
- Excessive neon glow, glass effects, gradients, or unrelated 3D icons.

### Iconography

- Use a consistent 2 px outline at a 24 px base grid.
- Use square or slightly rounded corners that echo the circuit mark.
- Active states may use Electric Teal; default states use Signal White or Clear Steel.
- Icons support a label; they do not replace unclear language.
- Replace the current website service emojis with one coherent set.

### Graphic devices

- Circuit nodes and paths.
- Numbered system steps: `01`, `02`, `03`.
- Thin grid lines and terminal-style labels.
- Cropped teal rule or corner marker.
- Before → build → result diagrams.

Use no more than two devices in one composition.

### Motion

- Prefer short node connections, path traces, state changes, and clean reveals.
- Standard UI motion: 160–240 ms.
- Campaign/reel transition: 300–500 ms.
- Avoid constant pulsing, long decorative intros, and motion without meaning.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` on the website.

## 7. Verbal Identity

### Voice essence

Sosa Tech sounds like a technically capable partner who explains the problem clearly, builds the useful solution, and never hides behind jargon.

### Tone dimensions

| Dimension | We are | We are not |
|---|---|---|
| Expert | Specific and experienced | Condescending or overly technical |
| Direct | Clear about the problem and next step | Abrupt or careless |
| Practical | Focused on useful outcomes | Trend-chasing |
| Confident | Calm and evidence-led | Loud, inflated, or absolute |
| Human | Conversational and bilingual | Slang-heavy or unprofessional |

### Voice examples

**Do:** “Every new lead should receive a useful response in minutes, not tomorrow.”  
**Don't:** “Revolutionize your omnichannel customer-acquisition ecosystem.”

**Do:** “We connect your form, WhatsApp, CRM, and follow-up so your team does not copy the same information four times.”  
**Don't:** “Our game-changing AI integrations guarantee explosive growth.”

**Do:** “First we map the process. Then we automate the parts that waste time.”  
**Don't:** “AI can automate everything.”

### Writing rules

- Lead with the business problem or desired result.
- Explain the tool only when it helps understanding or credibility.
- Use active voice and concrete verbs: build, connect, capture, respond, measure, improve.
- Keep most sentences under 22 words.
- Use contractions in conversational marketing copy.
- Use numerals for steps, time, percentages, prices, and measurements.
- Use the Oxford comma in English.
- Use one exclamation point at most, and rarely.
- Do not write entire paragraphs in all caps.
- Write **Sosa Tech Solutions** on first reference and **Sosa Tech** afterward.

### Bilingual rules

- Translate the intent, not the word order.
- Keep technology product names unchanged: WhatsApp, Meta, Google, n8n, Docker, Cloudflare.
- Prefer common Miami/Latin American Spanish understood across countries.
- Use **tú** for social and website marketing copy unless a formal client context requires **usted**.
- Avoid awkward Anglicisms where a natural Spanish phrase is clearer.
- Review Spanish separately; never publish an unreviewed automatic translation.

### Vocabulary

**We say**

- Practical systems
- Clear next step
- Built around your workflow
- Faster follow-up
- Fewer manual tasks
- Reliable infrastructure
- Direct support
- Measure what matters
- Start with the problem
- Built to be maintained
- English/Español
- We build. You grow.

**We don't say**

- Revolutionary
- Disruptive
- Game-changing
- Guaranteed growth
- Fully autonomous
- Magic
- Effortless
- One-size-fits-all
- Synergy
- Cutting-edge solution (without specifics)
- AI-powered everything
- Set it and forget it

## 8. Messaging System

### Core message

Technology that captures leads, saves time, and keeps your business moving.

### Value proposition

Sosa Tech Solutions builds connected websites, automation, AI, advertising systems, and infrastructure for small businesses. Clients work directly with a bilingual technical partner and receive a clear scope, maintainable implementation, and a system tied to an actual business result.

### Tagline

**We build. You grow.**

Use the tagline as a closing statement, cover line, footer, or campaign signature. It should not replace the more specific headline that explains what is being built or who it helps.

### Key messages and proof requirements

1. **Capture every opportunity.** Connect websites, ads, WhatsApp, and lead intake. Show the actual lead journey or response-time improvement.
2. **Remove repetitive work.** Automate handoffs, reminders, routing, and reporting. Show a real workflow or before/after process.
3. **Build on reliable foundations.** Improve websites, servers, tracking, and streaming. Show uptime, speed, monitoring, or deployment evidence only when verified.
4. **Work directly and clearly.** Provide bilingual communication, transparent scopes, and practical handoff. Show the process, founder, or client feedback.

Never invent statistics to fill a proof block. Use screenshots, diagrams, process reductions, or qualitative testimonials until verified numerical outcomes exist.

### CTA library

**Primary**

- Book a free systems review.
- Show us where your process gets stuck.
- Let's map your next system.

**Secondary**

- Message us on WhatsApp.
- See how the workflow works.
- Read the practical guide.
- Save this checklist.

### Short descriptions

**One sentence**  
Sosa Tech Solutions builds websites, automation, AI, and reliable infrastructure for Miami small businesses.

**Social bio**  
Automation, websites & AI for small businesses. Miami · English/Español. We build. You grow.

**Spanish short description**  
Sosa Tech Solutions crea sitios web, automatizaciones, IA e infraestructura confiable para pequeños negocios en Miami.

## 9. Brand in Use

### Website

- Lead with a business result and a named audience.
- Display the three primary offers before specialist capabilities.
- Use one primary CTA per viewport.
- Place proof close to the claim it supports.
- Use Electric Teal for actions and selected states, not long body copy.
- Include visible Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and contact links.
- Use real service pages and case studies for search and conversion.

### Social media

- Use icon-only artwork for profile images.
- Keep cover and thumbnail hooks to 3–8 words when possible.
- Use one visual idea and one CTA per post.
- Design the first carousel slide and reel cover to be understood without reading the caption.
- Maintain safe margins of at least 72 px on feed graphics and keep reel essentials away from interface overlays.
- Captions may use English first and Spanish second with a clear separator.
- Use accurate subtitles on all spoken reels.

### Caption structure

1. Hook: name the problem or surprising truth.
2. Value: 2–5 short points or one clear explanation.
3. Proof/context: what Sosa Tech sees, built, or recommends.
4. CTA: one useful next action.
5. Spanish version when applicable.

### Email

- Use plain HTML or clean text with the full logo no larger than 180 px.
- Keep one main CTA.
- Signature order: name, role, Sosa Tech Solutions, phone/WhatsApp, website.
- Do not use motivational quotes, oversized social icons, or image-only signatures.

### Presentations and proposals

- One idea per slide.
- Use Signal White or Carbon backgrounds; teal marks the decision, result, or next step.
- Use real scope, timeline, owner, and acceptance criteria.
- Diagrams must have a left-to-right or top-to-bottom reading direction.

### Business cards and print

- Front: icon or primary logo with generous clear space.
- Back: name, role, phone, email, web, and a QR code to the tracked contact page.
- Proof all dark fills and teal reproduction before a production run.

## 10. Brand Don'ts Checklist

- [ ] Never redraw or AI-generate the logo.
- [ ] Never shrink the full wordmark below 160 px digital or 42 mm print.
- [ ] Never use unapproved colors or low-contrast color pairs.
- [ ] Never use Electric Teal for long text on a light background.
- [ ] Never use more than one major CTA in one social asset.
- [ ] Never publish an invented statistic, testimonial, interface, or client result.
- [ ] Never lead with unexplained technology when a business outcome is clearer.
- [ ] Never mix unrelated icon or illustration styles.
- [ ] Never publish automatic Spanish translation without review.
- [ ] Never allow generated imagery to render the brand name, logo, URL, or important copy.
