# Approved Social Assets

Place only assets that Victor has explicitly reviewed and approved in this
folder. Use the matching subfolder:

- `feed/` for posts and carousels
- `reels/` for final vertical videos
- `stories/` for final vertical Story frames and videos
- `profile-and-cover/` for profile, cover, and highlight assets

Moving a file here records creative approval only. It does not authorize or
trigger a Facebook or Instagram post. Public publishing still requires a
separate, current instruction and the publisher's explicit `--publish` flag.

These are public-ready assets. Because `content/assets/social/` is part of the
website deployment allowlist, files here may become publicly retrievable after
a future website deployment even when they are not linked from a page. Do not
store drafts, client-confidential material, credentials, or customer data here.
