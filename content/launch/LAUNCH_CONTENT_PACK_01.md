> No solo recomendamos herramientas. ### Allowed proof visuals

- Abstract workflow canvas with all client names and identifiers removed.
- Container-health layout using fictional names such as `web`, `automation`, and `stream`.

- Backup lifecycle: encrypted → off-site → verify → restore test.

### Prohibited proof visuals

- Credentials, `.env` content, access tokens, customer messages, phone numbers, chat IDs, client databases, real lead records, workflow URLs, or internal hostnames.
- Claims that a workflow increased revenue or response rate unless the measurement is documented.

**Format:** 6 slides

2. `It means the system can tell you when something is wrong.`

4. `The backup is tested—not just assumed.`
5. `There is a clear recovery path and owner.`

This is how we think about client infrastructure and our own operations.
