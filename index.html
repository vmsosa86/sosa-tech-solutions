Common Questions

Clear answers
before we build.

A useful first conversation should clarify the problem, ownership, timing, and next step.

What happens in the free systems review?

We map the current process, identify the main bottleneck, and decide whether a website, automation, campaign, or infrastructure change is the right next move. There is no obligation to continue.

Do you work in English and Spanish?

Yes. Discovery, implementation, documentation, and support can be handled in English or Spanish.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

Usually. We first evaluate the current stack and keep what is useful. A replacement is recommended only when it reduces risk, cost, or unnecessary work.

How long does a project take?

A focused improvement may take days; a connected multi-system build may take several weeks. The proposal defines deliverables, owners, dependencies, and acceptance criteria before work begins.

Who owns the accounts and systems?

Ownership is made explicit in the scope. Whenever practical, the client owns the business accounts and Sosa Tech receives only the access required to do the work.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. Support can include monitoring, maintenance, workflow updates, campaign operations, incident response, or a defined handoff to the client's team.
