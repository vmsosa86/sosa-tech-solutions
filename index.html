Questions before we build

Before we get started.

What happens in the free lead-flow review?

We map how inquiries arrive, who responds, what happens next and where opportunities become delayed or invisible. You receive a practical recommended next step with no obligation to continue.

Can you automate WhatsApp without making it feel robotic?

Yes. Useful automation handles capture, routing, reminders and status while leaving important conversations to people. The goal is better service, not replacing the relationship.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

We focus on owner-led service businesses, including beauty and wellness, travel and customer services, home services, professional offices and media operations.

Do you work in English and Spanish?

Yes. Discovery, implementation, documentation and support can be handled in English or Spanish.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

Usually. We keep useful tools and recommend replacement only when it meaningfully reduces risk, cost or manual work.

Can you create and schedule our social media content?

Yes. We can build a brand-aware workflow for ideas, bilingual copy, graphics and Reels, with your approval before scheduled publishing and a visible publication record.

Who owns the accounts and systems?

Whenever practical, your business owns its accounts. Sosa Tech Solutions receives only the access required to complete and support the work.
