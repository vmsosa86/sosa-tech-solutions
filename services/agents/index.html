Two agents, two jobs

One answers your customers. One creates your content.

They do different jobs, so each one is set up on its own, with its own rules. You decide what each agent can do and what comes back to you.

Agent 01 · Customer inquiries

The 24/7 inquiry agent

Answers your customers on WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook messages and your website, at any hour, with the information you approve.

What it does

  • Answers customer questions using only the information you approve.
  • Collects the customer’s details: name, what they need, and the date or estimate they’re asking for.
  • Qualifies and organizes each inquiry, so you know what it’s about before you reply.

What it hands to you

  • An organized inquiry with the customer’s details and request.
  • An alert when a conversation needs a person, so you or your team take over.

Agent 02 · Social media content

The content agent

Drafts posts, captions and AI-presenter videos for your business, so your Facebook and Instagram keep showing up.

What it does

  • Drafts posts and captions about your services, offers and news.
  • Prepares AI-presenter videos, labeled as AI.
  • Schedules what you approve on Facebook and Instagram, and you get a confirmation when it’s live.

What you approve

  • Every post, caption and video, before anything is published.
  • Any changes: ask for edits, and nothing moves forward without your OK.

FAQ

Clear answers
before we build.

Can it answer at 2 a.m.?

Yes. The inquiry agent answers at any hour with the information you approved and collects the customer’s details. If the conversation needs a person, you get an alert and pick it up when you’re available.

What happens when it doesn’t know?

We agree on that before launch: when it should ask the customer for more details and when it should pass the conversation to you or your team, with what it already collected. I test it with real questions from your business before it goes live.

Do I approve the content?

Yes, always. The content agent prepares drafts. You review every post, caption and video, ask for changes, and nothing is published until you approve it.

Do I keep my WhatsApp number?

The agent can work with your business’s existing WhatsApp where the provider allows it. Every setup is different, so we confirm it during the review, before anything changes.

Is this a CRM product?

No. Each agent is set up around specific tasks for your business. If you already use a CRM, we can assess whether it can be connected.

How much does it cost?

Every business needs a different setup, so I quote each one after the review. The proposal includes any ongoing platform costs before we start.
