FAQ

before we build.

Do you manage existing servers?

Yes, after a read-only assessment of access, services, backups, monitoring, exposure, and current ownership.

Is a backup enough?

No. A backup becomes useful only when it is protected, recent, verified, and connected to a tested restoration procedure.

Can you use Cloudflare and Docker?

Yes. We use them when they simplify security, routing, deployment, recovery, or maintenance for the actual system.
