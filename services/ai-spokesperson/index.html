FAQ

Clear answers
before your first video.

Will people know it’s AI?

Yes. I label it on screen and in the caption of every post. The goal is a clear, professional video, not to fool anyone.

Can the videos be in Spanish and English?

Yes. Spanish, English, or both versions of the same video, each written for its audience rather than translated word for word.

Who approves the script?

You do, always. Nothing is produced until you approve the script, and you see the finished video before it’s published.

Can you publish it for me?

Yes. I can schedule it on your Facebook and Instagram and confirm when it’s live. If you prefer to post it yourself, you get the final video files.

Do I need to record anything?

No. No camera, no studio and no recording on your side. If you have photos of your business, products or logo, I can use them in the video.
